Privacy

What we keep, and why.

We collect as little as we need to run your account and your store — and we never sell it.

If you have an account

  • Your Google name and email address, to sign you in.
  • Your stores, products, photos and designs, so they are there on every device.
  • Your membership status and dates.
  • Basic activity (for example when you signed up or exported a design), to keep the service working and improve it.

If you shop in a store

When you place an order, the store you buy from receives your name, mobile number, address, optional email and your order. We store it on the store's behalf so they can deliver it; the store owner is responsible for how they use it. Your bag and the details you typed are remembered in your own browser to save you typing next time.

Cookies

One cookie keeps you signed in. Stores count page views without cookies. We don't use advertising trackers.

Where it lives

Data is stored with Google Firebase and served through Cloudflare. Sign-in uses Google.

Your choices

You can ask us to export or delete your account and stores from the contact page.
