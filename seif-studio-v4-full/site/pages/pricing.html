Questions
What is free, exactly?

Signing in, building stores, adding products, editing themes, previewing your store and designing in the 3D studio — including exporting your designs. The membership is what puts your store online for shoppers and lets it take orders.

What happens if my membership ends?

Your store goes back to “opening soon” for visitors, and nothing is deleted: products, orders, customers and designs all stay. Renew and it's live again in a second.

How do I pay until online payment is ready?

Press “Request activation”. We get in touch with you, you pay by InstaPay or wallet transfer, and your membership is switched on — usually the same day.

Is there a commission on my sales?

No. You keep everything your customers pay you on delivery.
